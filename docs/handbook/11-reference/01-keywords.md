# Keywords

Core declarations and control words include `fn`, `gen`, `yield`, `class`, `interface`, `trait`, `record`, `enum`, `type`, `construct`, `share`, `import`, `use`, `mut`, `inmut`, `strict`, `if`, `else`, `match`, `with`, `for`, `in`, `while`, `do`, `loop`, `break`, `continue`, `return`, `try`, `catch`, `default`, `finally`, `unsafe`, `task`, `await`, `parallel`, `thread`, `abstract`, `implements`, `public`, `private`, and `protected`.

Reserved literals/types include `true`, `false`, `null`, `Void`, `Never`, `Null`, and `Object`. The formal lexer remains authoritative; historical words excluded from 1.x—such as `async fn`, `worker`, `comptime`, and general `defer`—are not usable features.
