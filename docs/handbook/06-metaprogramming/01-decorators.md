# Decorators

Decorators inspect authorized metadata and generate or transform syntax during compilation. Their public contract identifies accepted arguments, valid declaration targets, generated API, diagnostics, retained reflection, and compile permissions.

Every transformation is reparsed, resolved, typed, and validated; decorators cannot bypass the compiler.
